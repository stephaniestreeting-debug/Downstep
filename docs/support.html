Having trouble, or have a question or suggestion? Email stephaniestreeting@yahoo.com.au and we'll get back to you.

It's not picking up my breathing

Try propping your phone a little closer, or use headphones with a built-in mic — that keeps the mic near your mouth regardless of where the phone sits. If it's still not working, or you're somewhere noisy, switch "Input" to "Track by Touch" in the app — you'll get the same guided experience by dragging instead.

Can I use it without sound?

Yes — set "Sound" to "Silent" in the app. Everything else works exactly the same.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Downstep works fully offline.
