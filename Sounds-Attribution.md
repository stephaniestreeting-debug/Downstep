# Bundled atmosphere recordings

Rain is the only atmosphere using a real recording (Birds and Lofi remain
fully generated on-device — see AudioManager.swift). Wind and Ocean were both
tried as real recordings and removed — Wind read as harsh/gusty, and Ocean's
shore-lapping read as anxiety-inducing rather than calming. Sourced from
Pixabay under the Pixabay Content License: free for commercial use,
modification allowed, no attribution required, no account needed to obtain.
https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/

- `Downstep/Sounds/rain.m4a` — "Calming Rain Loop" by DRAGON-STUDIO, trimmed to
  the first 30s (original is 3:00). Replaces an earlier pick ("Heavenly Rain
  Sounds") that read as close/indoor ("like a shower") rather than outdoor rain.
  https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/nature-calming-rain-loop-398653/
